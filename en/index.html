I am a strategic technology and delivery leader based in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia. As Chief Technology Officer at Hyper Tech I own the technology vision, product delivery and operational execution behind two production SaaS platforms — Daamup for real estate and property operations, and HiLaw for corporate legal and governance workflows.

My work sits where business strategy meets engineering reality: turning ambiguous business needs into architecture, roadmaps and sprints that a team can actually deliver. I lead cross-functional teams of 15+ across development, design and marketing, manage budgets up to SAR 2M, and sit in the room for hiring decisions and executive client conversations.

Before stepping into the CTO seat I spent years as a project manager and software engineer — building banking and commercial systems, running Agile ceremonies, and shipping web and mobile products across healthcare, e-commerce, accounting and ERP. That path is why I can talk credibly to both a board and a backend team in the same afternoon.

Alongside my role I consult independently for founders and teams who need senior product and technology judgement without a full-time hire: MVP scoping, technical blueprints, architecture reviews, project rescue, and AI automation.
