The problem

Property operators in Saudi Arabia were running portfolios across spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and disconnected accounting tools. Nothing reconciled, nothing was auditable, and regulatory reporting was manual every single month.

What we built

Daamup consolidates the full property lifecycle — units and contracts, tenant onboarding, rent collection, maintenance tickets, and financial reporting — into one multi-tenant platform designed Arabic-first with full RTL support.

My role

I own the product and technology: architecture decisions, data model, integration strategy with government and payment systems, hiring and team structure, and the delivery cadence that keeps releases predictable.
