Privacy Policy

How enquiry data submitted through this website is handled.

What is collected

When you submit the contact form, this site stores the name, email address, phone number, company, selected service, budget, timeline and message you provide, along with your IP address and browser user agent for spam prevention.

How it is used

Enquiry data is used only to respond to your enquiry and to manage the resulting engagement. It is never sold, and it is not used for marketing lists.

Analytics

This site may use Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns. Analytics data is not linked to your enquiry.

Your rights

You can request a copy of the data held about you, or ask for it to be deleted, by emailing the address in the footer. Requests are actioned within 30 days.
